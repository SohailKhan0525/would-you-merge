# 🏆 Hall of Fame

Everyone who caught every bug. Add yourself!

**How**

1. Get all katas green in your fork. Your fork's `badges` branch has a `progress.json` with the proof.
2. Open **this file on the upstream repository**, click the ✏️ pencil, and add **one row** at the bottom of the table.
   (Editing from the upstream page makes GitHub create the branch for you, so you won't accidentally
   send your solved katas along. A PR that changes anything else will be asked to split it.)
3. Open the pull request. That's it.

It's an honour system, and nobody's checking your grader. The row is a high five, not a certificate.
